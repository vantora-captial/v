# Vantora Japan Consultation-First UX Redesign

Date: 2026-08-18
Branch: `trilingual-public-site`
Status: Direction approved in conversation; written-spec review pending

## 1. Goal

Redesign the Japanese public site as a Japan-native, consultation-first cross-border M&A website for Japanese business owners, shareholders, advisers, asset owners and project sponsors.

The Japanese site should no longer feel like a translated international advisory site. It should help a Japanese visitor quickly answer:

1. Is my situation something I can discuss with Vantora?
2. What types of matters can I bring?
3. How will Vantora help me think through options?
4. What overseas counterparties can potentially be considered?
5. How confidential and low-commitment is the initial discussion?

## 2. Strategic Direction

English and Traditional Chinese remain international boutique-advisory experiences.

Japanese becomes a Japan-market-specific UX while keeping the same Vantora brand identity, legal guardrails and technical platform.

Recommended positioning model:

> 課題解決・相談獲得型のクロスボーダーM&Aサイト

Not:

> 国際版を日本語に翻訳したサービス紹介型サイト

## 3. Reference Principles

The Japanese design direction takes inspiration from Japanese corporate and M&A websites that lead with understandable business problems, simple verbs, reassurance and consultation pathways rather than abstract capability language.

Reference principles to adopt:

- Start with the visitor's management issue, not Vantora's credentials.
- Use everyday Japanese before specialist financial language.
- Make `相談` the dominant conversion concept.
- Reduce psychological commitment at the first contact.
- Explain how work progresses in concrete stages.
- Use cross-border access as a differentiator after trust is established, not as the first message.
- Present expertise through concrete actions and process rather than unsupported scale claims.

Do not copy slogans, layouts or proprietary visual elements from reference sites.

## 4. Homepage Information Architecture

Japanese homepage order:

1. Hero: management issue + overseas options
2. `こんなお悩みはありませんか`
3. `Vantoraができること`
4. `海外との選択肢を広げる`
5. `案件をどう進めるか`
6. Selected experience / examples
7. Why Vantora / Japan-side support
8. Consultation reassurance
9. Contact

This replaces the current Japanese sequence that mirrors the international version.

## 5. Hero

Primary headline:

**会社や事業のこれからを、  
海外という選択肢から考える。**

Supporting copy:

> 事業承継、会社・事業の譲渡、資本受入れ、海外企業との提携。  
> Vantoraは、日本企業の次の選択肢を、海外の投資家・事業会社との対話から支援します。

Primary CTA:

**まずは相談する**

Secondary CTA:

**Vantoraについて**

Reassurance strip:

**初期相談可｜秘密保持に配慮｜案件名の開示不要**

The approved global positioning `日本におけるクロスボーダーM&A・戦略投資` may remain as a smaller eyebrow, metadata line or brand descriptor, but should not be the dominant Japanese hero headline.

## 6. `こんなお悩みはありませんか`

Use issue-first cards or rows rather than service categories.

Recommended items:

### 後継者について悩んでいる
会社や事業を次の世代へつなぐ方法を検討したい。

### 会社・事業の譲渡を検討している
国内だけでなく、海外の買い手も選択肢として検討したい。

### 海外から資本を受け入れたい
成長投資や新規事業のため、海外の資本・事業会社との提携を考えたい。

### 海外企業との事業提携を検討している
販路、技術、JV等、海外企業との新しい可能性を探したい。

### 不動産・エネルギー・事業案件について相談したい
BESS、データセンター、不動産、船舶等の案件を海外投資家に提案したい。

Each item should lead to a relevant consultation route.

## 7. `Vantoraができること`

Replace abstract capability-first language with three concrete verbs:

### 整理する
案件の状況、目的、条件を日本側で整理します。

### つなぐ
案件に応じて、海外の投資ファンド、ファミリーオフィス、上場企業を含む事業会社、戦略投資家等との対話を支援します。

### 進める
NDA、情報開示、条件協議、デューデリジェンス、取引実行まで支援します。

The detailed Capabilities page may still retain M&A, Energy, Data Center, Real Estate and Special Assets categories, but the homepage should not lead with those categories.

## 8. Overseas Differentiation

Heading:

**海外との選択肢を広げる**

Recommended body:

> 案件の内容・規模・業種・取引目的に応じて、海外の投資ファンド、ファミリーオフィス、上場企業を含む事業会社、戦略投資家等との協議を支援します。

Follow with Japan-side execution language:

> 単に相手候補を紹介するのではなく、案件整理、守秘、初期対話、条件協議、デューデリジェンス、取引実行まで日本側で支援します。

Do not display investor logos, counts, AUM or claims of guaranteed access unless separately approved and substantiated.

## 9. Process

Heading:

**案件をどう進めるか**

Six stages:

1. **初期相談** — まずは匿名・概要レベルから。
2. **案件整理** — 目的、条件、資料、取引スキームを整理。
3. **候補先の検討** — 案件に合う海外投資家・事業会社候補を検討。
4. **守秘・初期協議** — NDA締結後、必要な範囲で情報共有。
5. **条件協議・DD** — 条件整理、デューデリジェンス、関係者調整。
6. **取引実行** — クロージングまで日本側で支援。

This replaces the international `Identify / Evaluate / Engage / Structure / Execute` framing on the Japanese homepage.

## 10. Consultation Reassurance

Near the final CTA, use:

### まずはご相談ください

> 会社・事業の譲渡、資本受入れ、海外企業との提携、投資・事業案件について、初期段階からご相談いただけます。案件名や詳細情報を最初から開示いただく必要はありません。

Primary CTA:

**相談する**

Supporting reassurance:

**初期相談可｜秘密保持に配慮｜案件名の開示不要**

Avoid claiming formal privilege or legally guaranteed confidentiality unless a specific agreement is in place.

## 11. Visual Direction

Keep Vantora's dark navy / warm gold / ivory identity, but make the Japanese version visibly more Japan-native:

- smaller headline scale than EN/ZH
- more white/ivory background and less theatrical dark presentation
- more fine rules and editorial dividers
- slightly higher information density
- fewer English eyebrows
- shorter Japanese headings
- less rounded UI
- CTA styling should feel like a consultation desk, not an investment pitch
- reassurance labels should be visible but not promotional badges
- photography should favor business, industrial and corporate environments over dramatic tourist-style skyline imagery

Mobile should remain compact and readable, with consultation paths appearing early and CTA targets at least 44px high.

## 12. Japanese Navigation

Recommended top-level labels:

- ホーム
- ご相談内容
- 支援内容
- 案件事例
- Vantoraについて
- ご相談

Avoid overusing English labels such as `Capabilities`, `Private Discussion`, `Cross-Border Counterparties` in the Japanese UI.

## 13. Contact UX

The contact page should feel like an initial consultation desk.

Primary question:

**どのようなことをご検討ですか。**

Recommended routes:

- 会社・事業の譲渡
- 海外からの資本受入れ
- 海外企業との事業提携
- 投資・事業案件の相談
- 日本での買収・投資相談

If keeping only four technical route keys in V1, combine the last item with the capital/investment route while preserving existing form behavior.

Add a visible initial-consultation note:

> まだ方針が決まっていない段階でも構いません。現在の状況やお考えを伺いながら、考えられる選択肢を一緒に整理します。

## 14. About UX

The Japanese About page should explain how Vantora works rather than lead with abstract credentials.

Recommended sections:

1. Vantoraの役割
2. 日本側で行うこと
3. 海外との接点
4. 対象となる案件
5. 進め方と守秘

UPEX remains secondary as the Japan-side operating foundation.

## 15. Compliance and Evidence Boundaries

Preserve all existing public-claim guardrails.

Do not claim:

- guaranteed buyers or investors
- exclusive global network
- quantified family office network
- AUM or transaction volume
- named counterparties without approval
- regulated securities placement, investment management, brokerage or real-estate brokerage status without verification

Preferred wording:

- 相談
- 案件整理
- 候補先の検討
- 協議支援
- 条件協議
- 実行支援

## 16. Technical Scope

Keep the existing static generator and three-language routing.

Japanese UX may use dedicated Japanese templates/content composition instead of post-build string replacement. The current build-time `ja-sourcing` injection is considered transitional and should be replaced if practical so the Japanese page has a coherent source-of-truth structure.

Do not change English or Traditional Chinese content except where shared technical components require safe, non-visible refactoring.

Preserve:

- `/en/`, `/zh/`, `/ja/`
- canonical/hreflang
- browser-language routing
- Registry projection and confidentiality rules
- presentation/non-live opportunity labels
- AI Concierge
- direct email/phone
- Cloudflare preview isolation

## 17. Testing

Tests must verify:

- Japanese hero uses issue-first messaging.
- `こんなお悩みはありませんか` appears before detailed services.
- `整理する / つなぐ / 進める` are present.
- Japanese homepage uses six-stage Japanese process, not the international English process labels.
- consultation reassurance language is present.
- overseas funds, family offices, listed companies and strategic investors appear only as qualified counterparty categories.
- prohibited network/AUM/guarantee claims remain absent.
- EN/ZH remain unaffected.
- contact routing, SEO, Registry safety and AI Concierge behavior remain intact.

## 18. Acceptance Criteria

The redesign is successful when a Japanese business owner or adviser can understand within the first two sections:

1. Vantora is open to hearing about their situation even before a decision is made.
2. Company sale, succession, capital, overseas partnership and real-asset/project matters can be discussed.
3. Overseas investors and companies are an additional option Vantora can help explore depending on the case.
4. Vantora supports the Japan-side process, not just introductions.
5. Initial consultation can begin with limited information and with confidentiality in mind.
6. The page feels written and structured for Japanese corporate users, not translated from an international advisory site.

No production merge or deployment occurs before explicit user approval after preview review.
