# Japan Investment Hub V4 Conversion Design

## Goal
Rebuild the homepage around investor conversion and two-sided deal origination while preserving the existing UPEX project facts, transaction examples, multilingual support, and BATONZ external profile.

## Primary audiences
1. International institutional investors, family offices, strategic buyers, banks and listed companies seeking Japan opportunities.
2. Japanese project owners, companies and asset holders seeking international capital, buyers, JV partners or financing.

## Primary conversion
`Tell Us Your Investment Mandate` remains the primary CTA.

## Secondary conversions
- `Request Teaser / NDA` for specific projects.
- `Submit an Opportunity` for Japanese project owners.
- `View UPEX on BATONZ` as external professional reference.

## Homepage narrative
1. Hero: Invest in Japan — access opportunities, execute locally.
2. Investor / Project Owner dual entry: choose whether you are seeking opportunities or submitting one.
3. Current Opportunities: LNG, BESS, AI Data Center, Japanese Companies & M&A.
4. Featured strategic assets: LNG, BESS portfolio, AI Data Center.
5. Selected Transactions: JPY 500M, JPY 800M, JPY 1.2B anonymized UPEX cases.
6. Trust Layer: BATONZ external professional profile plus UPEX execution capabilities.
7. Japan Investment Intelligence: concise daily investor-relevant topics, not a generic news portal.
8. Final conversion: investor mandate + project submission + Tokyo contact.

## Product principles
- Lead with what the client can access, not with company history.
- Use real project parameters already supported by UPEX materials.
- Separate live/featured opportunities from general capabilities.
- Clearly label indicative LNG financing/charter figures.
- Avoid implying that BATONZ endorses UPEX projects.
- Do not claim daily website automation is live until it actually is.
- Use Manrope + Noto Sans SC/JP throughout.

## UI principles
- Institutional dark navy / warm ivory / restrained gold.
- Strong whitespace and large numeric project metrics.
- Reduce paragraph density.
- Add status chips and audience labels where useful.
- Use a sticky mobile conversion bar for `Investment Mandate` and `Submit Opportunity`.
- Keep responsive single-column behavior on small screens.

## Content architecture
### Dual entry
Investor:
- Looking for Japan opportunities
- CTA: Tell Us Your Investment Mandate

Project owner:
- Have a Japan asset, company, site, power resource or transaction opportunity
- CTA: Submit an Opportunity

### Current opportunity cards
Each card should answer:
- What is it?
- Scale / indicative metric
- Stage / type
- What action can the user take?

### Trust layer
- Selected Transactions first.
- UPEX execution capability second.
- BATONZ public adviser profile as external reference.

## Verification
- Existing LNG, BESS, AI Data Center, three transaction examples, UPEX contact details and BATONZ URL remain present.
- English, Chinese and Japanese language switching remains functional.
- Main CTA is Investor Mandate.
- A distinct Submit Opportunity CTA exists for Japanese-side origination.
- No false claim of BATONZ endorsement or live automated news publishing.
