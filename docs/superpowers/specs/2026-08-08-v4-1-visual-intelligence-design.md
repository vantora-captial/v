# Japan Investment Hub V4.1 Visual + Intelligence Design

## Goal
Upgrade the existing production website in place, preserving the V4 investor/project-owner conversion architecture while making the site feel more like an institutional Japan investment platform.

## Scope
1. Replace repeated generic grid imagery with asset-specific visual treatments for LNG shipping, BESS, AI data centers and Japan M&A.
2. Improve the hero and opportunity cards so each investment theme is recognizable within seconds.
3. Rework Japan Investment Intelligence into concise investor briefing cards rather than a generic news list.
4. Preserve Investor Mandate and Submit Opportunity as the two core conversion paths.
5. Preserve UPEX transaction cases, Tokyo contact details and BATONZ external profile.

## Visual Direction
- Institutional, restrained, premium.
- Dark navy base, warm ivory content surfaces, restrained gold accent.
- Manrope + Noto Sans SC / Noto Sans JP.
- Large metrics and short labels over long paragraphs.
- Asset-specific visual panels with clear captions and no implication that illustrative imagery is the actual asset unless verified.

## Homepage Changes
### Hero
Keep `Invest in Japan. Access opportunities. Execute locally.` as the primary proposition. Add a compact market strip emphasizing Energy Infrastructure, Digital Infrastructure, Japanese Companies and Cross-border Capital.

### Opportunity Cards
Each card receives a visual identity and three pieces of scannable metadata: asset type, scale, and investor action.

### LNG
Use a dedicated maritime visual treatment and retain the 174,000 CBM / five-vessel / indicative financing and charter assumptions with the existing disclaimer.

### BESS
Use a dedicated battery/grid visual treatment. Keep the 2MW -> 100MW portfolio narrative and distinguish high-voltage aggregation from strategic extra-high-voltage opportunities.

### AI Data Center
Use a dedicated data-center infrastructure visual treatment. Keep 200MW+ as the core development scale and emphasize land + power + local execution requirements.

### M&A
Use Tokyo/Japan corporate imagery or an abstract transaction visual, while keeping the three anonymized transaction examples as the evidence layer.

## Japan Investment Intelligence
Turn the section into six briefing cards grouped under Energy & BESS, LNG & Maritime, AI & Data Centers, Japanese Companies, Cross-border M&A, and Policy & Capital. Each card explains what investors should watch and why it matters. Do not claim automatic daily publishing is live. Keep a transparent note that automation is planned / being connected.

## Trust
Keep Selected Transactions before Why UPEX. Keep BATONZ as an external professional profile and explicitly avoid endorsement language.

## Conversion
Primary: Tell Us Your Investment Mandate.
Secondary: Submit an Opportunity.
Project-specific: Request Teaser / Discuss Investment.
Mobile: retain sticky two-action conversion bar.

## Integrity Rules
- Do not fabricate live deal status, returns, counterparties, project locations or transaction details.
- Do not represent illustrative visuals as actual project photography unless verified.
- Preserve all current disclaimers around indicative LNG figures.
- Preserve UPEX Tokyo address, phone and email currently on the production site.
- Preserve English, Chinese and Japanese language switching.

## Verification
- Existing V4 dual-audience gateway remains.
- LNG, BESS, AI Data Center and M&A remain the four primary opportunity themes.
- Repeated generic grid image is removed from asset-specific feature panels.
- Intelligence section is visibly more scannable and does not claim live automation.
