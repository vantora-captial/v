# Vantora Editorial Advisory Visual Redesign

Date: 2026-08-17
Branch: `trilingual-public-site`
Status: Visual direction approved in conversation; written-spec review pending

## 1. Objective

Refine the existing trilingual Vantora public website so it visually reads as a boutique cross-border M&A / strategic investment advisory firm rather than a generic consulting or software site.

The redesign uses the user-supplied desktop/mobile reference image as the visual benchmark for hierarchy, density, image-led composition and institutional tone, while retaining the already-approved Vantora positioning, trilingual architecture, Project Registry safety boundaries and current public information architecture.

The goal is not to copy the reference literally. The goal is to reproduce its strongest characteristics in the Vantora system:

- deep navy + warm gold institutional palette
- image-led hero with a strong Japan business context
- compact, high-trust navigation
- dense but orderly editorial information hierarchy
- white / ivory content sections separated by fine rules
- strong Selected Opportunities presence
- coordinated desktop and mobile brand experience
- premium financial-advisory rather than generic corporate-web styling

## 2. Positioning That Must Remain Unchanged

The redesign is visual-first. It does not revert to the old positioning visible in the reference image.

Primary English hero remains:

**Cross-Border M&A & Strategic Investment in Japan**

Supporting copy remains focused on target identification, Japan-side dialogue, transaction coordination and execution.

Traditional Chinese and Japanese retain their separately written approved positioning.

Do not restore generic phrases such as:

- `Japan Opportunities. Local Execution. Global Perspective.` as the main hero
- `Who We Work With` as the homepage's dominant second section
- generic `Capital Advisory` or `Sector Advisory` wording that weakens the six confirmed capability pillars

M&A remains the strongest narrative and visual axis.

## 3. Visual Character

Target impression:

> Boutique cross-border advisory with credible Japan-side access and transaction execution capability.

The visual language should feel closer to an investment bank, private capital advisory firm or institutional transaction boutique than to a SaaS product, generic agency or lifestyle brand.

### 3.1 Palette

Core palette:

- very dark navy / midnight blue for header, hero overlays and trust sections
- warm ivory / off-white for the primary reading surface
- restrained muted gold for active states, rules, numbering, icons and primary CTAs
- cool slate / blue-grey for secondary text
- minimal use of pure black or saturated colors

Gold is an accent, not a fill color for large surfaces.

### 3.2 Typography

Use a strong serif / editorial display treatment for primary headlines and a clean sans-serif system for navigation, labels, metadata and body text.

Headline typography should carry authority and editorial rhythm without becoming ornamental.

English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese should be tuned independently for line-height and wrapping. Do not force identical line breaks across languages.

## 4. Header and Navigation

The header should visually resemble the reference's compact institutional navigation.

Desktop:

- logo / Vantora identity aligned left
- restrained primary navigation centered / right-weighted
- permanent EN / 中文 / 日本語 switch visible in the main header
- thin active-language gold underline or equivalent refined state
- no oversized navigation buttons
- sticky behavior permitted if visually discreet

Mobile:

- Vantora brand remains visible
- compact language switch remains visible
- menu icon at far right
- header height approximately 64–70px
- no collision with AI Concierge launcher

Brand hierarchy remains Vantora-first. UPEX may appear as a secondary endorsement but should not dominate the top-left identity as `UPEX / Vantora` unless an explicit later branding decision changes this.

## 5. Homepage Composition

Homepage section order remains:

1. Hero
2. Core Capabilities
3. Selected Experience
4. Selected Opportunities
5. How We Execute
6. Why Vantora
7. Private Discussion

The redesign changes visual treatment, not information architecture.

### 5.1 Hero

The hero becomes a strong image-led first viewport inspired by the reference.

Desktop:

- large Japan business / urban / infrastructure image spanning the hero
- dark directional overlay to protect text contrast
- hero copy aligned left in a controlled content column
- headline large but not oversized
- one gold primary CTA and one restrained outline / text secondary CTA
- image should suggest Japan business execution, not tourism

Preferred image themes:

- Tokyo / Japan business skyline at dusk or blue hour
- high-quality urban infrastructure / financial district perspective
- optional infrastructure / industrial imagery only if it still supports M&A positioning

Avoid:

- cherry blossoms
- temples as primary business imagery
- tourist Tokyo clichés
- generic stock-business handshake photography

Mobile:

- image remains dominant in first viewport
- headline line breaks are curated by language
- body copy shortened visually through width / spacing, not rewritten into unsupported claims
- CTAs stack vertically

### 5.2 Core Capabilities

Do not imitate the old four-column `What We Do` block literally.

Use a compact editorial strip / indexed module inspired by the reference's density, but preserve the six confirmed pillars:

1. Cross-Border M&A
2. Energy & Infrastructure
3. AI Data Center & Digital Infrastructure
4. Commercial & Industrial Real Estate
5. Special & Real Assets
6. Japan Market Entry & Strategic Partnerships

Cross-Border M&A receives larger type, stronger rule / number treatment or a lead position.

The remaining five should feel connected but subordinate.

### 5.3 Selected Experience

Use a refined editorial list rather than large repeated cards.

Each item should show:

- sector / engagement type
- concise title
- one or two lines of scope
- current stage / qualitative status where useful

No client logos, invented transaction values or fabricated success outcomes.

### 5.4 Selected Opportunities

This becomes the strongest visual section after the hero.

Inspired by the reference, opportunity modules should use:

- real or approved illustrative image area
- concise asset / transaction category
- public title
- 1–2 short metadata lines
- restrained `View Opportunity` / `Request Details` CTA

Desktop may use 3–4 visual opportunity modules in a row or a large lead + secondary arrangement.

Mobile should use highly scannable stacked opportunity rows/cards with image + title + metadata, similar in density to the supplied reference but with Vantora's approved content and non-live sample labels in preview mode.

Presentation samples must remain visibly identified as non-live.

### 5.5 How We Execute

Convert the current five-stage process into a more editorial horizontal / numbered sequence on desktop:

01 Identify
02 Evaluate
03 Engage
04 Structure
05 Execute

Use fine rules, gold indices and concise copy.

Mobile becomes a vertical numbered sequence.

### 5.6 Why Vantora

Use a dark navy trust band inspired by the reference's `Why` section.

Retain only the three approved reasons:

- Japan-side access
- Cross-border transaction perspective
- Execution-led advisory

Optionally include a small UPEX operating-foundation note beneath or adjacent to these reasons.

Do not add unverified network, performance, return, AUM or exclusivity claims.

### 5.7 Private Discussion

Use a strong closing section with an image / dark visual counterweight, similar to the reference's lower-page editorial composition.

Primary goal: direct visitors into the four-path Private Discussion flow.

Keep direct email and phone visible.

## 6. Interior Pages

The reference image should influence the whole site, not only the homepage.

### Capabilities

- dark / image-led intro band
- editorial index and section rules
- less card-heavy layout
- M&A visually first

### Experience

- case-study style reading experience
- numbered / ruled rows
- strong sector labels and execution scope

### Opportunities

- image-led listing
- institutional filter treatment
- more compact card density
- clear public/private boundary
- presentation-only sample warning remains visible in preview

### About

- large editorial statement
- restrained Vantora / UPEX relationship explanation
- optional Japan business-context image

### Contact / Private Discussion

- split editorial layout on desktop
- four pathway choices prominent
- simpler single-column mobile form / mail-draft experience

## 7. Image Strategy

The redesign requires stronger image art direction.

Use images only where they add transaction / sector context:

- Japan business skyline / urban infrastructure
- BESS / energy storage
- data center / digital infrastructure
- maritime / aviation where relevant
- industrial / commercial real estate
- boardroom / professional environment as secondary imagery

Do not expose real confidential project imagery unless the project file is explicitly approved for public use.

For preview / non-live opportunities, use clearly illustrative imagery and never imply that an image depicts the actual live asset.

Image treatment should be consistent:

- cool / slightly desaturated grading
- controlled contrast
- no overly vivid stock-photo look
- clean crop ratios
- no decorative image mosaics without purpose

## 8. Desktop Density

Compared with the current implementation, reduce excessive whitespace and increase useful information density.

Target:

- more information visible per viewport
- narrower vertical section gaps
- stronger horizontal alignment
- fine divider rules between editorial modules
- fewer oversized rounded cards
- lower border radius overall
- more rectangular, investment-report-like modules

The site must remain readable and premium rather than crowded.

## 9. Mobile Design

The supplied mobile reference is a key benchmark.

Mobile homepage priority:

1. compact institutional header
2. image-led hero
3. primary CTA
4. capability / audience signal
5. Selected Opportunities
6. remaining advisory proof sections

Although the information architecture still retains Capabilities and Experience before Opportunities in source order, mobile styling may compress those preceding sections so Opportunities appear quickly in the scroll.

Mobile opportunity modules should emphasize image + title + transaction category + CTA.

Do not shrink desktop layouts mechanically. Build deliberate mobile compositions.

## 10. Interaction and Motion

Motion should remain subtle:

- restrained fade / translate on first reveal only if it does not harm performance
- no parallax-heavy hero
- no autoplay carousel
- no rotating financial metrics
- hover states through underline, border, image scale <= 1.02, or gold accent

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 11. Existing Functional Requirements Preserved

The visual redesign must not regress:

- `/en/`, `/zh/`, `/ja/` standalone routes
- browser-language routing
- persisted manual language choice
- canonical / hreflang SEO
- sanitized Project Registry public projection
- hidden-price behavior
- non-live presentation sample labelling
- AI Concierge integration
- direct email / telephone contact
- responsive / accessibility contracts
- public-output secret / private-field audit

## 12. Technical Direction

Continue using the current static-site generator architecture:

- `site/content.mjs` for copy / content data
- `site/templates.mjs` for page structure
- `assets/site.css` for visual system
- `assets/site.js` for progressive interaction
- `assets/opportunities.mjs` for sanitized public opportunity rendering
- Node build / test workflow

Avoid introducing a framework solely for the visual redesign.

The implementation should be predominantly template / CSS / image asset work, plus targeted tests for new structural classes / mobile behavior.

## 13. Testing Requirements

Add / update tests to verify:

- hero image-led layout hooks exist on all three homepages
- M&A remains first and visually primary
- Selected Opportunities image modules render safely
- presentation-only opportunity labels remain visible
- no private Registry fields are exposed
- language switch remains visible on mobile
- no horizontal overflow hooks at 375px / 390px
- reduced-motion behavior exists
- desktop and mobile navigation remain accessible
- AI Concierge does not collide with mobile navigation / primary CTA
- generated public output remains free of secrets and unsupported claims

Existing full frontend + Worker CI must remain green.

## 14. Preview and Release Boundary

Implement on the existing isolated `trilingual-public-site` branch or a visual-redesign child branch if isolation is needed.

Deploy only to the Cloudflare Pages preview environment.

Do not merge to `top` and do not replace the production site until the user visually reviews the redesigned desktop and mobile preview and explicitly approves production integration.

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

The redesign is accepted when:

1. The site visually reads as a boutique M&A / strategic investment advisory firm within the first viewport.
2. The hero has the image-led authority and density of the supplied reference while using Vantora's current positioning.
3. M&A remains the dominant capability.
4. Selected Opportunities has strong visual presence without becoming a marketplace.
5. Desktop information density feels closer to an investment advisory report / institutional site and less like a generic card-grid website.
6. Mobile retains the same premium brand hierarchy and surfaces Opportunities early.
7. English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese all remain native in tone and readable in their own typographic rhythm.
8. No confidential project data, unsupported claims or regulatory overstatements are introduced.
9. Existing AI, Registry-safety, SEO and language-routing behavior remains intact.
10. The redesign is delivered first as an isolated preview; production remains unchanged until explicit approval.
