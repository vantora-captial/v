# Vantora Public Site Redesign V2 — A+C Design Specification

Date: 2026-08-16
Branch: `ai-sales-redesign`
Status: User-approved direction; implementation pending

## 1. Strategic Direction

The second redesign combines two roles:

- **A — Premium Tokyo investment / M&A advisory:** the brand must feel credible, discreet, senior, and institutionally polished.
- **C — Japan opportunities & resource-access platform:** visitors must also feel that Vantora can surface relevant investment, M&A, infrastructure, partnership, and market-entry opportunities.

The design must not look like a generic consulting brochure or a public project marketplace. The intended impression is:

> **Boutique cross-border advisory with privileged Japan access and the ability to execute locally.**

The site should communicate both trust and opportunity, with conversion paths built around private discussions and curated opportunity access.

## 2. Problems to Correct from V1

The current preview has four primary issues:

1. **Image system is off-positioning.** The images do not sufficiently express premium Tokyo business access, local execution, and transaction credibility.
2. **Information architecture is too neutral.** The page explains what Vantora is but does not pull a visitor through a strong commercial journey.
3. **Language is too institutional and descriptive.** It lacks direct value propositions, urgency of relevance, and persuasive next-step guidance.
4. **Opportunity content feels detached from the advisory brand.** It must function as evidence of access, not as a product catalogue.

V2 must resolve all four together rather than treating copy, imagery, or CTA design as isolated fixes.

## 3. Positioning

### Primary brand

**Vantora** remains the sole primary visual brand.

### Endorsement

Use **Powered by UPEX Tokyo** sparingly, primarily in About/Why Vantora and the footer.

### Positioning statement

Vantora is a **Tokyo-based cross-border investment, M&A, and strategic access platform** connecting international investors and companies with selected Japan opportunities, local counterparties, and execution support.

### What the visitor should understand within 10 seconds

- Vantora is based in Tokyo.
- Vantora helps international investors and companies access Japan.
- Vantora provides both advisory/execution capability and access to selected opportunities.
- The next step is to explore opportunities or start a private discussion.

## 4. Audience Priority

Primary audiences:

1. Overseas investors, funds, family offices, and strategic capital.
2. Overseas companies entering Japan, acquiring in Japan, or seeking local strategic partners.

Secondary audience:

3. Japanese companies and project owners seeking international buyers, investors, or partners.

The homepage should visually and verbally prioritize the first two groups.

## 5. Conversion Strategy

V2 is designed as a **high-trust conversion website**, not a brochure.

Every section should answer one visitor question:

1. **Hero:** Why should I pay attention?
2. **Who We Help:** Is this relevant to me?
3. **What We Unlock in Japan:** What can Vantora actually help me obtain or execute?
4. **Selected Opportunities:** What evidence is there that Vantora has access?
5. **Why Vantora:** Why trust this team?
6. **Selected Experience:** What kinds of situations can they handle?
7. **Private Discussion:** What is the next step?

### Primary conversion actions

Use two recurring actions:

- **Explore Opportunities**
- **Book a Private Discussion**

Avoid internal terms such as “mandate,” “ticket size,” and “submit your opportunity” in prominent public CTAs.

## 6. Homepage Structure

Final order:

1. Hero
2. Who We Help
3. What We Unlock in Japan
4. Selected Opportunities
5. Why Vantora
6. Selected Experience
7. Private Discussion / Contact

**Insights / News is removed from the primary homepage flow** or reduced to a small optional footer-level link. It must not interrupt the conversion journey.

### 6.1 Hero

Purpose: establish authority, access, and relevance immediately.

Hero should use a premium Tokyo business/city visual and clear cross-border positioning.

Recommended headline direction:

**Access Japan Through Trusted Local Execution**

Alternative supporting line:

**Private Opportunities. Strategic Access. Cross-Border Execution.**

Supporting paragraph should state in plain language that Vantora helps international investors and companies identify, access, structure, and execute opportunities in Japan.

Primary CTA:

**Explore Opportunities**

Secondary CTA:

**Book a Private Discussion**

No sector tags, project metrics, or third CTA in the hero.

### 6.2 Who We Help

Keep this concise and commercially framed.

Groups:

- Investors, Funds & Family Offices
- Overseas Companies Entering Japan
- Japanese Companies & Project Owners

Each group should describe the business outcome, not just the audience category.

Examples:

- Access selected Japan investment and acquisition opportunities.
- Build market entry, partnership, and local execution pathways.
- Reach international capital, buyers, and strategic partners.

### 6.3 What We Unlock in Japan

This replaces a generic “What We Do” service list.

The wording must focus on what the client can gain access to or execute.

Recommended pillars:

- **Investment Access** — selected Japan investment and infrastructure opportunities.
- **M&A & Strategic Acquisitions** — target sourcing, transaction support, negotiation, and local coordination.
- **Market Entry & Partnerships** — local partners, distribution, JV, commercial alliances, and execution support.
- **Capital & Strategic Matching** — international investors, buyers, or strategic counterparties for Japanese opportunities.

Where applicable, deeper descriptions may mention structuring, due diligence coordination, negotiation, and PMI, but only where accurate.

### 6.4 Selected Opportunities

This section should create curiosity and demonstrate access.

Opportunity categories may include:

- LNG & Energy Logistics
- Japan BESS & Grid Infrastructure
- AI Data Centers & Digital Infrastructure
- Japanese Companies & Cross-Border M&A

Presentation rules:

- Do not make the section resemble an e-commerce/product grid.
- Use fewer, larger cards with stronger editorial imagery.
- Avoid unsupported returns, exact availability, live counterparties, confidential locations, or implied guarantees.
- If numerical information is not verified for public disclosure, use qualitative framing.
- Each card CTA should feel private and selective, e.g. **View Opportunity Theme**, **Request Details**, or **Discuss This Sector** rather than “Buy / Apply / Invest Now.”

The section should explicitly frame these as **selected themes / opportunities available through Vantora’s network and advisory process**, subject to verification and suitability.

### 6.5 Why Vantora

Purpose: convert interest into trust.

Core themes:

- Tokyo-based local execution
- Cross-border investment and M&A experience
- Senior, hands-on support
- Access to local business networks and counterparties
- Multilingual communication
- Confidential, private process

Use “Powered by UPEX Tokyo” here as a supporting endorsement.

Do not overstate network size, exclusivity, licenses, regulatory status, or transaction volumes.

### 6.6 Selected Experience

Use qualitative case-type examples unless verifiable public transaction details exist.

Recommended presentation:

- Cross-border M&A / succession / carve-out support
- Japan market-entry and strategic partnership execution
- Infrastructure / energy / digital project coordination

Avoid fabricated deal values, AUM, logos, account numbers, certifications, or transaction metrics.

### 6.7 Private Discussion / Contact

This must be a deliberate conversion section rather than a generic contact block.

Recommended heading:

**Start a Private Discussion in Tokyo**

Supporting copy:

Discuss your Japan investment, acquisition, market-entry, or strategic partnership objectives with our Tokyo-based team.

Primary action:

**Book a Private Discussion**

Secondary option:

Direct email / telephone contact.

AI assistant may remain available as a secondary tool but must not be the primary conversion path until fully production-verified.

## 7. Language Strategy

The biggest V2 copy change is moving from “describing Vantora” to “showing why the visitor should act.”

### English

Tone:

- senior
- concise
- commercially persuasive
- high-trust
- clear international business English

Preferred language:

- access
- selected opportunities
- local execution
- strategic acquisition
- market entry
- private discussion
- cross-border execution

Avoid:

- vague corporate slogans
- excessive financial jargon
- hard-sell investment language
- startup/tech clichés

### Simplified Chinese

Chinese must be written as a business-development website, not translated institutional English.

Preferred value framing:

- 进入日本市场
- 获取日本投资与并购机会
- 寻找本地合作方 / 收购标的 / 项目资源
- 对接资本、买家与战略合作伙伴
- 东京本地执行支持
- 私密沟通 / 一对一讨论

Recommended CTA examples:

- **查看日本精选机会**
- **预约一对一私密沟通**
- **与东京团队讨论您的日本计划**

Avoid overused marketing phrases such as “赋能全球资本生态” or literal translations of “mandate.”

### Japanese

Japanese should prioritize trust, discretion, local execution, and business outcomes.

Preferred framing:

- 日本市場への参入
- 投資・M&A案件へのアクセス
- 現地パートナー探索
- 買収候補・事業機会の探索
- 海外投資家・戦略パートナーとの連携
- 東京での実行支援

Recommended CTA examples:

- **日本の投資・M&A機会を見る**
- **個別相談を予約する**
- **日本での事業・投資戦略を相談する**

## 8. Visual Direction

### Overall character

The visual system should communicate:

- premium Tokyo advisory
- discreet institutional access
- selected opportunity visibility
- cross-border execution

Do not make the site look like an investment crowdfunding portal, public listing platform, or generic corporate consulting template.

### Palette

Retain:

- deep navy
- warm ivory
- restrained gold

But increase editorial contrast and reduce repetitive boxed-card treatment.

### Layout

Use:

- larger image-led editorial blocks
- stronger asymmetry on desktop
- fewer cards per row
- more white space
- clearer hierarchy

Avoid four equal-weight tiles immediately after each section heading.

## 9. Image System

V2 images must serve positioning, not decoration.

### Hero

Use a premium Tokyo business-district or skyline image that feels international and executive.

Target locations / visual references:

- Marunouchi
- Otemachi
- Toranomon
- Tokyo waterfront business skyline
- modern Tokyo corporate architecture

Avoid tourist imagery, generic Shibuya crowds, cherry blossoms, or a single infrastructure asset as the brand hero.

### Selected Opportunities

Each opportunity category should use realistic industry imagery:

- LNG: vessel, terminal, logistics, port infrastructure
- BESS: modern battery storage, grid, clean energy infrastructure
- AI Data Center: actual data-center or computing infrastructure
- M&A: Japanese corporate / industrial / executive business environment

Images must not imply that a pictured asset is the exact opportunity being marketed unless verified.

### Why Vantora / Experience

Use premium Japan business imagery: executive meeting, corporate district, industrial site visit, transaction context, or Tokyo office setting.

Avoid cliché handshakes, fake holographic AI graphics, posed call-center teams, and low-end stock imagery.

## 10. Mobile Direction

Mobile remains a first-class target.

- Hero title should stay materially smaller than desktop.
- CTA buttons should stack cleanly.
- First screen should show positioning + at least one CTA without excessive scrolling.
- Opportunity cards should become image-led vertical editorial blocks.
- No overly large section titles.
- Chinese and Japanese line breaks must be reviewed independently.

## 11. Truthfulness / Compliance Guardrails

Do not publish or imply:

- guaranteed returns
- “exclusive” opportunities unless substantiated
- current live counterparty names without permission
- precise project details that are confidential or unverified
- AUM, client count, certifications, licenses, transaction values, or account numbers without evidence
- regulatory or investment-advisory claims beyond actual status

Public opportunity content should remain informational and subject to verification / suitability where appropriate.

## 12. AI Sales Relationship

The site must convert without AI.

AI remains a secondary interaction layer. It must not replace:

- Explore Opportunities
- Book a Private Discussion
- direct contact

Full production prominence should wait until OpenAI quota, multilingual live responses, compliance boundaries, and email lead delivery are verified.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria

V2 is ready for preview when:

1. Hero clearly communicates Tokyo-based investment/M&A access within one screen.
2. Homepage combines premium advisory identity with opportunity access without looking like a marketplace.
3. New section order is: Hero → Who We Help → What We Unlock in Japan → Selected Opportunities → Why Vantora → Selected Experience → Private Discussion.
4. Insights/News no longer interrupts the main homepage conversion path.
5. English, Chinese, and Japanese are rewritten for commercial conversion, not literal translation.
6. Primary CTAs are **Explore Opportunities** and **Book a Private Discussion** or natural language equivalents.
7. Hero image is premium Tokyo business imagery.
8. Selected Opportunities use sector-specific editorial imagery and fewer/larger cards.
9. Unsupported metrics and claims remain removed.
10. Mobile hero, typography, and CTA hierarchy remain restrained and usable.
11. Standard contact remains usable independently of AI.
12. Preview deployment remains isolated from `top` until explicit user approval.
