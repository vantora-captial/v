# BATONZ Credential Link Design

## Goal
Add the user's public UPEX BATONZ adviser page to the Japan Investment Hub as a third-party professional-profile link that strengthens M&A credibility without implying BATONZ endorsement of UPEX projects or investment opportunities.

## Placement
Place a compact credential card in the `Why UPEX` section, after the six execution capabilities and before the Contact section. This keeps third-party credibility close to UPEX's execution story without distracting from the primary LNG, BESS, AI Data Center and Investor Mandate conversion paths.

## Card Content
English:
- Label: `EXTERNAL PROFESSIONAL PROFILE`
- Heading: `M&A Advisory in Japan`
- Copy: `View UPEX's public adviser profile on BATONZ, a Japanese M&A platform.`
- CTA: `View UPEX on BATONZ →`

Chinese:
- Label: `第三方专业平台资料`
- Heading: `日本 M&A Advisory`
- Copy: `查看 UPEX 在日本 M&A 平台 BATONZ 上的公开顾问资料。`
- CTA: `查看 UPEX 的 BATONZ 页面 →`

Japanese:
- Label: `外部プロフェッショナルプロフィール`
- Heading: `日本のM&Aアドバイザリー`
- Copy: `日本のM&Aプラットフォーム BATONZ に掲載されているUPEXの公開アドバイザープロフィールをご覧いただけます。`
- CTA: `BATONZのUPEXページを見る →`

## Link Behavior
- Destination: `https://batonz.jp/partner_adviser/upex/pg2888641.html`
- Open in a new browser tab with `target="_blank"`.
- Use `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- Do not use wording such as `endorsed`, `certified`, `recommended by BATONZ`, or any language implying that BATONZ validates UPEX's LNG, BESS, AI Data Center or investment projects.

## Visual Treatment
Use the existing institutional dark-navy/gold system and Manrope/Noto Sans typography. The credential should look like a restrained external-profile card rather than an advertisement or partner logo wall. Include a small `BATONZ` text mark only; do not copy or recreate BATONZ trademark artwork unless a licensed asset is available.

## Conversion Priority
The BATONZ card is secondary proof. The primary CTA remains `Tell Us Your Investment Mandate`; the BATONZ link must not visually compete with that action.

## Verification
- Confirm the exact BATONZ URL exists in `index.html`.
- Confirm `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"` are present.
- Confirm English, Chinese and Japanese translations are present.
- Confirm no endorsement language is used.
