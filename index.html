Japan BESS Project Center

Energy storage projects from development to operating assets.

UPEX supports Japanese high-voltage and extra-high-voltage battery energy storage projects, typically from 2MW / 8MWh and above, through sourcing, acquisition, development, financing and asset management.

Early DevelopmentLand, grid-connection rights and connection-study materials. ID/land transfer or project-company acquisition.
Mid DevelopmentConstruction contribution, permits and EPC plan. Development-right transfer or joint development.
ConstructionEPC, equipment, finance and schedule. Equity investment or construction financing.
Operating / CODOperating assets, market revenue and O&M contracts. Equity acquisition, portfolio purchase or refinancing.
